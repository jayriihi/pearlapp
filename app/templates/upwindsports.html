Upwind Sports I started Upwind Sports in 2016 to help folks enjoy windsurfing in Bermuda. By 2019 we had discovered foiling—first through windsurf foiling, and soon after through wing foiling (or wing-dinging, as we like to call it). What began as curiosity quickly turned into an all-consuming pursuit. Foiling opened up a whole new range of conditions and fundamentally changed how we thought about wind, water, and time on the board. Being part of this shift has been an incredible adventure. During quieter periods, non-windy days, and especially through the COVID years, that same curiosity found a new outlet: wind data and web development. Our aim was simple and practical—to give local sailors, including ourselves, accurate, real-time wind and tide information to help choose the right gear and make the most of every session. That effort has grown into a dedicated wind-information project for Bermuda. Our latest live site is now up and running. Built by sailors, for sailors, we hope you find it genuinely useful.
